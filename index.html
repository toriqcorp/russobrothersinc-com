Our Story

Two brothers, one truck, and a promise to do right by drivers.

Russo Brothers Inc was founded by two brothers who grew up around diesel engines and freight docks and decided Hartford needed a carrier that still answers the phone. What started as a single truck and a handshake has grown into a tight-knit fleet that runs the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Midwest — but the family-shop mentality never left.

Every driver who runs under our authority is treated like family, because around here, that's not a slogan — it's how the Russos were raised to do business. Newer equipment, honest dispatch, and a seat at the table when it matters.
